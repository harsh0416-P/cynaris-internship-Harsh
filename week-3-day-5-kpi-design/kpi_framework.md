# Week 3 — Day 5: KPI Design

## Business Domain: Retail Sales

This KPI framework helps a retail business measure
sales performance, profitability, and customer activity.

## KPI 1: Total Revenue

- Formula: Sum of all sales revenue
- Owner: Sales Manager
- Frequency: Daily
- Target: ₹5,00,000 per month
- Type: Lagging Indicator

## KPI 2: Revenue Growth

- Formula: (Current Revenue - Previous Revenue) / Previous Revenue * 100
- Owner: Sales Manager
- Frequency: Monthly
- Target: At least 10% growth
- Type: Lagging Indicator

## KPI 3: Gross Profit

- Formula: Revenue - Cost of Goods Sold
- Owner: Finance Manager
- Frequency: Monthly
- Target: ₹1,00,000 per month
- Type: Lagging Indicator

## KPI 4: Gross Profit Margin

- Formula: (Gross Profit / Revenue) * 100
- Owner: Finance Manager
- Frequency: Monthly
- Target: At least 25%
- Type: Lagging Indicator

## KPI 5: Average Order Value

- Formula: Total Revenue / Number of Orders
- Owner: Sales Manager
- Frequency: Weekly
- Target: At least ₹2,000
- Type: Lagging Indicator

## KPI 6: Conversion Rate

- Formula: (Number of Orders / Website Visitors) * 100
- Owner: Marketing Manager
- Frequency: Weekly
- Target: At least 3%
- Type: Leading Indicator

## KPI 7: Customer Retention Rate

- Formula: (Returning Customers / Previous Customers) * 100
- Owner: CRM Manager
- Frequency: Monthly
- Target: At least 80%
- Type: Leading Indicator

## KPI 8: Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC)

- Formula: Marketing Spend / Number of New Customers
- Owner: Marketing Manager
- Frequency: Monthly
- Target: At most ₹500
- Type: Lagging Indicator

## KPI 9: Inventory Turnover

- Formula: Cost of Goods Sold / Average Inventory Value
- Owner: Inventory Manager
- Frequency: Monthly
- Target: At least 4 times per year
- Type: Lagging Indicator

## KPI 10: Stockout Rate

- Formula: (Out-of-Stock Products / Total Products) * 100
- Owner: Inventory Manager
- Frequency: Weekly
- Target: At most 2%
- Type: Leading Indicator


## Dashboard Insights

- Total Revenue: ₹400,000
- Gross Profit: ₹108,000
- Gross Profit Margin: 27%
- Number of Sales Records: 12

Revenue and gross profit help monitor financial performance.
Gross profit margin shows the percentage of revenue remaining
after costs. Regional and product charts help compare sales
performance across segments.

Note: These figures describe the sample dataset, not actual
business results. Average revenue per record should not be
treated as average order value without order-level data.
