**Executive Summary**

The analysis of 200 customers generating Rs 4,992,621 in total revenue reveals clear concentration of performance across geography, acquisition channel, and age segment. **City performance** is led by Pune (Rs 1,102,350), followed by Mumbai (Rs 926,771) and Hyderabad (Rs 847,312); the remaining five cities each contribute between Rs 620k and Rs 720k. **Channel performance** shows the App as the top source of revenue (Rs 1,468,234), with Offline (Rs 1,299,449), Website (Rs 1,242,438) and Marketplace (Rs 982,500) trailing. **Age‑group revenue** is highest among customers aged 45‑54 (Rs 1,365,174) and 55+ (Rs 1,075,519), while the 18‑24 cohort still delivers a solid Rs 1,002,911.

Churn is low: 6 customers have left, representing a **3 % churn percentage** of the base. The average revenue per customer is Rs 24,963 and the average order count per customer is 11.54, indicating relatively strong per‑customer value.

**Recommendations**  
- **Prioritize growth in Pune and Mumbai** through localized marketing and partnership initiatives, leveraging the strong existing revenue base.  
- **Invest in the App channel**, which outperforms other acquisition sources, by enhancing user experience and expanding in‑app promotions.  
- **Target high‑value age segments (45‑54 and 55+)** with tailored product bundles and loyalty programs to deepen spend.  
- **Implement a focused retention program** aimed at the 3 % churned segment, using win‑back offers and proactive service outreach to prevent further attrition.  
- **Balance channel mix** by testing offline and marketplace tactics that can capture additional share without cannibalizing the high‑performing App channel.

These actions align with the observed revenue distribution and aim to sustain growth while minimizing churn.
