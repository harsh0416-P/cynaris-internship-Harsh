
# Week 11 Day 1 – Data Storytelling Principles

## Project Overview
This project applies exploratory data analysis (EDA) and data storytelling principles to an Indian business dataset.

The objective is to transform raw data into meaningful findings and communicate them through clear visualizations and business recommendations.

## Objectives
- Inspect and understand the dataset.
- Identify and handle data quality issues.
- Analyze numerical and categorical variables.
- Explore distributions, correlations, and outliers.
- Communicate findings using the What – So What – Now What framework.
- Develop evidence-based business recommendations.

5. Correlation and outlier analysis
6. Business insight generation
7. Data storytelling and recommendations

## Storytelling Framework
- **What?** What patterns or findings were identified?
- **So What?** Why are those findings important?
- **Now What?** What actions should the business consider?
