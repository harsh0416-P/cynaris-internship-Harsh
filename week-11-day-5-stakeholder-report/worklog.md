\### Interaction 1

\*\*Question:\*\* What makes a business insight actionable for stakeholders rather than just a statistical observation?



\*\*Key takeaways:\*\*

\- Connect insights to a business problem.

\- Give clear, specific recommendations.

\- Quantify potential impact.

\- Assign ownership and timelines.

\- Define measurable success KPIs.



\*\*Learning:\*\* Actionable insights turn data into decisions through context, recommendations, measurable impact, and follow-up.

\### Interaction 2

\*\*Question:\*\* How should I communicate limitations in a dataset when presenting business recommendations?



\*\*Key takeaways:\*\*

\- Clearly state the dataset's scope and limitations.

\- Quantify uncertainty where possible.

\- Explain how limitations affect recommendations.

\- Provide mitigation steps for data gaps and biases.

\- Keep the limitations section concise and stakeholder-friendly.



\*\*Learning:\*\* Transparent communication of limitations builds stakeholder trust and helps prevent overconfident decisions.
