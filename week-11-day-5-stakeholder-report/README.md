\# W11D5 — Stakeholder Report: Five Business Insights

Perform exploratory data analysis on the Indian Business Dataset and communicate five evidence-based business insights to stakeholders.

\- Records: 600

\- Columns: 15

\- Cities: 8

\- Property types: 7

\- Missing values: 0

\- Duplicate listing IDs: 0

\- Date range: January 2023 – December 2024
